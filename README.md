# Garden Guardin'
Your peaceful garden is being ransacked by zombies! Thankfully you can shoot stars from presumably your hands, so you can protect your flowers. But also your flowers give you powers and healing? So what are we doing all this for really?

## Controls
WASD or Arrow Keys: Move

E: Shoot

R: Deploy Bomb 

You have an infinite number of bullets, but only a limited amount of bombs

Poppies heal you, but there are only a few around the map 

Zombies have different strength and scoring based on their colour (pink are stronger but give more points)
